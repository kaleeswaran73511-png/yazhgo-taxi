Frequently Asked Questions

What are the per-km rates?

Sedan: ₹15 one-way, ₹14 round trip. SUV: ₹20 and ₹19. Innova Crysta: ₹24 and ₹23. Round trips have a 250 km daily minimum.

Are tolls, parking and permits included?

Rates include the vehicle, fuel and driver batta. Fastag tolls, parking and interstate permits are paid at actuals with receipts.

How do I pay?

UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), cash to the driver, net banking, or cards after your ride.

How soon do I get driver details?

Within 15 to 30 minutes of booking, or 2 hours before pickup for advance bookings.
