FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers to your outstation travel and tariff queries.

Are there any hidden return charges for one-way drops?

Absolutely zero return charges! For all one-way drops, you pay strictly for the actual distance from your pickup point to your destination. We never charge for the vehicle's return journey.

What are the exact per-km rates and Driver Batta?

Our official tariffs are:
• Sedan Prime (Dzire, Etios): One-Way ₹15/km | Round Trip ₹14/km | Batta ₹400/day
• Spacious SUV (Innova, Ertiga): One-Way ₹20/km | Round Trip ₹19/km | Batta ₹400/day
• Innova Crysta VIP: One-Way ₹24/km | Round Trip ₹23/km | Batta ₹400/day
*Toll gate fees, parking, and interstate permits are billed at actuals with genuine receipts.

How does round-trip billing and minimum 250 km coverage work?

For round trips, billing has a standard minimum coverage of 250 km per calendar day. If your trip is 180 km in 1 day, billing is for 250 km. If you travel 320 km, you pay for exactly 320 km at the discounted round-trip rate.

How do I receive driver and taxi details after booking?

Once you submit your booking, our automated dispatch system registers it instantly and sends driver details (driver name, phone number, vehicle number) directly to your WhatsApp and SMS at least 2 hours before scheduled pickup.
