Frequently Asked Questions

Details regarding per-km rates, round-trip coverage, tolls, and cancellations.

What are the per-km rates for one-way drops vs round trips?

Our official tariffs are:
• Sedan (Dzire, Etios): One-Way ₹15/km | Round Trip ₹14/km
• SUV (Innova, Ertiga, Xylo, Marazzo): One-Way ₹20/km | Round Trip ₹19/km
• Innova Crysta: One-Way ₹24/km | Round Trip ₹23/km
*Round trips have a standard minimum coverage of 250 km per calendar day.

How does the 250 km daily round trip coverage work?

For round trips, billing is calculated based on total kilometers traveled or a minimum of 250 km per day (whichever is higher). If your 1-day trip is 200 km, billing will be for 250 km. If you travel 320 km, you pay for exactly 320 km at the discounted round-trip rate.

Are tolls, parking, and state permits included?

Our rate includes the vehicle, fuel, and driver batta. Fastag toll charges, parking fees, and interstate permit taxes (if crossing state borders) are paid strictly at actuals with genuine receipts.

What payment methods do you accept?

You can pay comfortably via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Cash to driver, Net Banking, or Credit/Debit Cards upon completing your ride.

How quickly will I receive my driver details?

Once you submit your booking, our automated system dispatches it directly to our operations team via Telegram & WhatsApp. You will receive vehicle number and driver phone details within 15 to 30 minutes (or 2 hours prior for advance bookings).
